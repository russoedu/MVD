ms-sans-serif-bold.woff2 is the FontStruction "MS Sans Serif" (bold) by "lou",
as converted to woff2 by 98.css (https://github.com/jdan/98.css). Its licence
is ms-sans-serif-license.txt (CC BY-SA 3.0) and FontStruct asks for
ms-sans-serif-readme.txt to travel with it; both are copied from 98.css
unchanged.

"MS Sans Serif" is the name of Microsoft's typeface; this is an independent
pixel recreation and is not made or endorsed by Microsoft.
